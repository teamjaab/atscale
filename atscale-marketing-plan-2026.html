Five structuring pillars, ordered from most urgent to most long-term. Each pillar is attributed to the entities it directly impacts.

P0 — Foundation

Web Ecosystem & Digital Presence

Build a coherent, conversion-focused digital ecosystem for each business unit while establishing a unified brand architecture at group level.

  • Operating Partners site refresh (standalone, no talent/training mention)
  • Talent multi-page site on talent.atscale.co (Candidate/Client tracks)
  • Training foundation site on training.atscale.co (methodology focus)
  • Unified naming convention and brand alignment across all touchpoints

P0 — Credibility

Proof Points & Authority Content WeSype-powered

WeSype's AI agent acts as the content production engine across the full editorial chain. JAAB orchestrates strategy, editorial direction, tone of voice and performance tracking. The agent handles volume and consistency.

  • LinkedIn posting routines for Partners Agent: 1 associate · 1 theme · 1 persona · 1 topic per post cycle
  • White papers and long-form thought leadership pieces Agent
  • Client proof points and success stories (text-first, video-adapted later)
  • Promotional video production: testimonials, BU showcases, format adaptations
  • LinkedIn performance KPIs: reach, engagement rate, follower growth, lead attribution per partner and per thematic
  • Monthly social analytics review and content calendar adjustment
Operating Partners Talent Training
Q2 → Q3 2026
3
P1 — Acquisition

SEO, Outbound & Lead Generation

Build predictable, scalable acquisition channels independent of the founders' personal networks, for each business unit.

  • SEO via WeBurst: technical audit, continuous optimization, monthly reporting
  • Systematic lead magnet approach on all WeSype-produced content (white papers, posts, case studies)
  • Thematic landing pages with tracking and conversion optimization
  • LinkedIn Ads experimentation to amplify top-performing content
Operating Partners Talent
Q3 → Q4 2026
4
P2 — Authority

Events & Industry Presence

Establish a physical and intellectual presence in PE/VC and B2B SaaS ecosystems to generate warm leads and reinforce legitimacy.

  • Speaker Program: pitching Partners for keynote or workshop slots (SaaStock, IPEM, London Tech Week)
  • Event partnership or booth as a meeting hub for clients and candidates
  • On-site activation: dedicated landing pages and QR-tracked content for attendees
  • Post-event content repurposing for digital channels
Operating Partners
Q4 2026 → Q1 2027
5
Ongoing — Governance

External CMO & Strategic Oversight

Transversal marketing leadership: strategy, prioritization, partner orchestration (WeBurst, WeSype, video studios) and full performance ownership.

  • Quarterly review with Partners: pipeline, SEO, reach, ROI
  • Social media monitoring: LinkedIn analytics per partner, per theme, per format
  • Single point of contact managing all external partners
  • Brand consistency across all touchpoints and entities
  • Ongoing editorial direction and quality control on WeSype-generated content
Group (transversal)
June 2026 → June 2027
💡
Core growth model: Authority-Led Growth. High-value content (case studies, insights, expertise) is the primary growth driver. WeSype provides the production scale. Acquisition layers (SEO, outbound) are amplifiers — not primary drivers. JAAB ensures the editorial standard, the strategic coherence, and the performance tracking.
